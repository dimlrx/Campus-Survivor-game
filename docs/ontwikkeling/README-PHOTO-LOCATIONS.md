# Campus Survivor - Photo locations and Jumbo update

The five photographs supplied by the user replace the illustrated location scenes for Saxion, Studentenflat, Centrum, Oude Markt and Jumbo. Kennispark keeps the old drawn interior until a photo is provided.

- Saxion: supplied Saxion interior photo
- Studentenflat: supplied photo of furniture outside a building (reference scene, not a real flat interior)
- Centrum: supplied Van Heekplein exterior photo
- Oude Markt: supplied evening cafe/street photo
- Jumbo / Werk: supplied supermarket interior photo

Inside each building, the mission panel shows options 1, 2 and 3. In the Studentenflat, option 4 sleeps for eight hours. Press Q to exit. On the outdoor map, the shop is now labelled JUMBO / WERK. Work hints mention Jumbo.

Photos are bundled under src/main/resources/interiors. They are the user-supplied reference photos, not 3D interiors. Photo rendering uses LibGDX Texture and SpriteBatch.

## Run
Windows: `gradlew.bat run`
macOS/Linux: `./gradlew run`

## Validation
The ZIP and photo assets were verified. Full Gradle compilation and live visual testing are not confirmed.
