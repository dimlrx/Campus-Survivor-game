# Student flat sleep and mission controls

Enter the Studentenflat with E. Inside, the displayed choices are missions 1, 2 and 3, plus **[4] Sleep in your bed (8h)**. Press 4 to sleep. Sleep is only offered inside the Studentenflat. Missions work by pressing 1, 2 or 3 directly (no M modifier). Press Q to exit. Outdoors, the original location-based activity shortcuts remain available near their entrances.
