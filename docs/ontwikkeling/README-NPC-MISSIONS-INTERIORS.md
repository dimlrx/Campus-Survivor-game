# Campus Survivor — NPC dialogues, missions & themed interiors

This update preserves SaxionGameApp, Gradle, the 2.5D geographic-inspired map, and the Sprint 0–3 systems.

## New content
- Four named NPCs (Tim, Noor, Sam, Alex), each with a branching three-stage conversation and three choices per stage. Choices affect stats; conversations provide small rewards.
- Six enterable locations, each with three unique, one-time missions (18 total). Missions require energy, can cost money, advance time, and alter student stats. Completed missions are marked DONE.
- Expanded, individually themed 2.5D interiors: Saxion study/reception, student flat/kitchen, Kennispark innovation lab, city shopping arcade, Oude Markt cafe, and workplace/shop.

## Controls
- WASD: move outside
- E: enter building at door or speak to nearby NPC
- 1/2/3: choose dialogue answer or complete mission while inside
- Q: close dialogue or exit building
- V: switch outdoor camera
- P: pause
- ESC: menu

## Limitations
These interiors are artistic, original game interpretations, **not** verified copies of actual building interiors. The outdoor map is approximate, not imported OpenStreetMap geometry. Build was not verified here because the Gradle distribution download was unavailable.

## Camera controls
The V camera/view toggle has been removed. The game uses the standard top-down view.
